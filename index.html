Privacy Policy

Van Park UK respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Van Park UK mobile application.

Information We Collect

Van Park UK may collect the following information:

  • Location data – used to show nearby van-friendly parking spots. Location access is optional and can be disabled in your device settings.
  • User-submitted content – such as suggested parking spots or feedback submitted through the app.

How We Use Information

Collected data is used solely to:

  • Provide core app functionality
  • Display relevant parking information
  • Improve app accuracy and coverage

We do not sell or rent your data.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties. Map services may be used to display locations.

Data Security

All data is transmitted securely using encrypted connections.

Children’s Privacy

Van Park UK is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
